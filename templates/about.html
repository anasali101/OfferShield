Security & limitations

What OfferShield can — and cannot — verify.

A cybersecurity tool should be honest about its own limits. This page explains the methodology, the evidence model, how uploaded documents are handled, and what remains unknown.

Methodology

Every scan combines independent, mostly deterministic checks. The AI layer is optional and advisory. It cannot create findings, change points or override the score.

01

Rule-based text and payment analysis

Pattern rules look for candidate-facing fees, training-kit charges, deposits, placement-confirmation payments, guaranteed placement, no-interview offers, urgency, sensitive-document requests, personal UPI or bank transfers, gift cards and crypto. Salary and stipend mentions are explicitly not treated as payment demands.

02

Document analysis

PDFs are parsed with PyMuPDF. We extract text, metadata, page count, embedded files and active-content markers. A SHA-256 file fingerprint detects exact duplicates. Hashed word shingles and RapidFuzz estimate similarity against previously indexed documents. Scanned PDFs use OCR only when Tesseract is installed; otherwise we report that the document could not be read.

03

Domain and website intelligence

We check RDAP/WHOIS registration age, DNS records, TLS certificate validity, redirects and URL patterns. We compare the registrable domain with a built-in list of known company domains using homoglyph normalisation and RapidFuzz similarity. Domain age and HTTPS are single signals, never verdicts.

04

Company verification

We compare the claimed brand with recruiter email domains, flag free consumer email domains and brand mismatches, and verify email/site domain consistency where possible. We never mark a recruiter as verified just because a company exists. MCA (India) master-data confirmation requires a manual CAPTCHA-based search and is reported as unverified.

05

Deterministic scoring

Points are assigned only to detected indicators and capped per category. Duplicate indicator IDs are counted once. The AI does not influence the score. Missing information never automatically becomes evidence of fraud.

Scoring model

CategoryMaximum pointsTypical evidence
Payment fraud indicators25Registration/training/deposit requests, personal UPI, bank transfer, gift cards
Domain & website intelligence20New domain, lookalike brand, punycode, TLS failure, shortener
Company verification20Brand/email mismatch, free email, email/site mismatch
Offer letter analysis15Exact duplicate, high similarity, active content, metadata anomaly
OSINT & reputation10Optional VirusTotal / Google Safe Browsing verdicts
Recruitment message patterns10Guaranteed placement, no interview, urgency, unrealistic pay

Interpretation: 0–24 low detected risk, 25–49 moderate warning signs, 50–74 high risk, 75–100 critical risk. An additional Insufficient evidence status is used when the score is low but too many verification checks could not be completed. A score is a count of detected indicators, not a probability of fraud.

Document and data handling

Uploaded PDFs

Processed in memory. The file is not written to disk, not executed and not retained permanently. Only extracted findings, short evidence snippets and a file hash are kept.

Reports

The investigation result is stored temporarily (default 24 hours) so you can view and download the PDF report. Expired cases are deleted automatically.

Similarity index

Only SHA-256 hashes of normalised word 5-grams are stored, never the offer-letter text. The index is pruned according to the configured retention period.

Optional AI

Disabled unless an API key is configured and you tick the consent box. Uploaded PDFs are never sent to the AI provider. Only derived findings and summaries are sent. For pasted messages, a redacted excerpt may be included.

Security controls

  • File signature, extension and size validation; safe filename handling; no execution of uploads.
  • SSRF protection: only http/https, blocked credentials-in-URL, blocked private/loopback/link-local/reserved addresses, restricted ports, re-validation on every redirect, connection timeouts.
  • No automatic download of page bodies; only status and headers are inspected.
  • Output escaping, strict Content-Security-Policy, anti-framing, nosniff and referrer-policy headers.
  • Cross-origin analysis submissions rejected; no API secrets in source code; environment-driven configuration.
  • Safe error handling that does not expose stack traces or user content.

Known limitations

  • No registry verification: MCA and similar registries require manual, CAPTCHA-protected searches. We show “Unverified”, not a fabricated result.
  • No vacancy confirmation: We cannot confirm that a real vacancy exists or that a sender is an authorised employee.
  • Brand list is finite: The built-in reference list cannot cover every company. Absence of a similarity match is not a positive signal.
  • Reputation lag: New scam domains often have no reputation history. A clean reputation result is not proof of legitimacy.
  • Domain age and HTTPS are weak signals: Legitimate new businesses exist; phishing sites routinely use valid certificates.
  • DNS rebinding residual risk: We re-validate DNS before requests and disable automatic redirects, but no in-process SSRF guard is perfect.
  • OCR depends on Tesseract: If the binary is missing, scanned documents are explicitly reported as not analysed.
  • AI can be wrong: The explanation is advisory, may contain errors, and is never used for scoring.
  • No rate limiting by default: Put OfferShield behind a reverse proxy with request limits for public deployments.

Optional integrations

All are configured through environment variables and are disabled by default. See .env.example and the README.

  • AI explanation: any OpenAI-compatible chat-completions endpoint (AI_API_KEY, AI_BASE_URL, AI_MODEL).
  • VirusTotal URL reputation (VIRUSTOTAL_API_KEY).
  • Google Safe Browsing (SAFE_BROWSING_API_KEY).
  • RDAP endpoint override (RDAP_URL_TEMPLATE).

Report immediately if you paid

In India, report cyber financial fraud at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930. Contact your bank to freeze or dispute the transaction.

Safe verification habits

  • Type the company domain manually; do not trust links.
  • Use the official careers portal or published switchboard.
  • Never pay for registration, training, equipment or a deposit.
  • Never share OTPs, CVV, passwords or full ID documents.
  • Ask for the offer on company letterhead plus an official email from the corporate domain.

Not a fraud determination

OfferShield reports indicators and missing evidence. It does not accuse a named individual or company of a crime. Always use independent judgment and official channels.
