How it works

Three ways in. One evidence-backed report.

Every investigation returns the same structure: score, findings, evidence, uncompleted checks and next actions.

Domain & website intelligence

Payment fraud detector

Separates candidate-facing fees from salaries and stipends. Detects UPI, bank transfer, gift-card and crypto payment instructions with severity.

Company verification

Compares claimed brands with recruiter email domains, flags free-email recruiters and brand mismatches. MCA registry search is disclosed as manual.

Example pattern

“Pay ₹2,000 for the training kit to confirm your placement.”

This single sentence contains several independent indicators: a candidate-facing payment, a guaranteed outcome, and a payment tied to placement confirmation. OfferShield scores each one separately, caps the payment category at 25 points, and explains the evidence.

  • Payment request detected — critical indicator
  • Guaranteed placement — high-risk recruitment pattern
  • Company identity — unverified unless independently traceable
